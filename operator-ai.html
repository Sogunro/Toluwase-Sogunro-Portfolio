OperatorAI

Crestline Plumbing & Heating — SMB Operations Assistant (POC)

Executive Summary

What this project is

OperatorAI is a multi-agent AI operations assistant that acts as a 24/7 intelligent front office for SMBs. It captures every customer enquiry across all channels, qualifies leads with real pricing, books appointments directly into Google Calendar, handles complaints with empathy, and syncs everything to HubSpot CRM — all without human intervention.

The first vertical is plumbing & heating. The architecture is built to scale across trades, restaurants, estate agents, and professional services.

The Problem

SMBs lose 40–60% of leads to inaction

A plumber on a job can’t answer the phone. An email sits in the inbox for 3 days. A website enquiry gets forgotten. By the time someone responds, the customer has moved on.

For a business like Crestline Plumbing & Heating (Manchester, UK, 6 staff, ~£400k/year turnover), every missed lead is real revenue lost.

Capabilities

What the system does

Instant Lead Capture
Answers customers instantly across web chat, Telegram, and email — no missed leads, 24/7.
Intelligent Lead Qualification
Extracts service needs, property details, location, timeline, and provides accurate pricing. Gates booking behind 4 required details.
Real Appointment Booking
Checks live Google Calendar availability and confirms slots directly in the conversation — no back-and-forth.
Complaint Handling
Acknowledges frustration, gathers details, escalates to the business owner when appropriate. Empathy-first approach.
Emergency Detection
Gas leaks, burst pipes, and vulnerable persons trigger immediate safety protocols with emergency numbers.
Cross-Session Memory
Recognises returning customers across channels and sessions by phone, email, or Telegram ID. Loads full history automatically.
CRM Auto-Sync
Creates contacts, deals, tickets, and quotes in HubSpot automatically — zero manual data entry.
Response Verification
A critic agent checks all draft responses against evidence before they reach the customer. Weakest-claim scoring.
Architecture

8 specialised agents

Gateway
Entry point — loads history, detects greetings/continuations/workflows, builds customer profile. No LLM.
Supervisor
Orchestrator — builds the LangGraph state graph, routes between agents. No LLM.
Intent Classifier
Gate-based pipeline with LLM fallback. Classifies into 8 intent types with urgency and lead scoring.
Contact Collector
Extracts phone/email via regex, appends natural contact requests when details are missing.
Lead Qualification
Sales agent — extracts lead data, provides pricing, gates booking behind 4 required details, has booking tools.
Customer Support
Answers FAQs from knowledge base, handles bookings/cancellations via tools, manages complaints.
Critic
Quality gate — verifies every draft response against RAG evidence, conversation history, and tool results before sending.
Multi-Intent
Splits compound messages like “Fix my tap and how much for a new bathroom?” into sub-intents, processes each, stitches responses.
Design Rationale

Key technical decisions

Single DB for State + Vectors
Neon PostgreSQL with pgvector handles both conversation state and RAG vector search. No separate vector database — simpler infra, lower cost, one connection pool.
Deterministic Gates Before LLM
6 regex-based gates (safety signals, ticket lookup, resume detection, continuity, multi-intent hints) run before LLM fallback. Catches emergencies in milliseconds.
Critic for Hallucination Prevention
Every response passes through verification against 5 evidence sources. Verdict determined by weakest claim, not average — one fabricated detail means “revise.”
Backend = Brain, n8n = Hands
The backend thinks, decides, validates, and stores. n8n receives messages, sends replies, and syncs to external tools. Neither does the other’s job. No overlap.
Conversation Compression
When conversations exceed 8 turns, older messages are summarised into 2–3 sentences while the last 6 are kept verbatim. Preserves context without exhausting the context window.
Cross-Session Recognition
Returning customers recognised across channels by phone, email, or Telegram ID. Name, address, service history, deal status, and past interactions loaded automatically.
