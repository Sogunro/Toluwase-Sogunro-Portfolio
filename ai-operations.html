Executive Summary

What this project is

A production, multi-channel agentic AI system that answers calls, handles email, books appointments, runs campaigns, and reports to the owner — built end to end and live for a real UK healthcare and patient transport business.

The domain is non-emergency patient transport, serving elderly, disabled and SEND passengers and hospital patients. These are vulnerable people, so accuracy and safety are non-negotiable: the system must never invent a price, a policy, or availability, and must escalate anything sensitive to a human.

The Problem

Losing customers to missed calls, unable to justify the payroll

A growing UK patient-transport business was losing customers to missed calls and slow email replies, but could not justify hiring a receptionist, an admin, a support rep, and someone to chase leads — thousands per month in payroll before serving one extra customer.

The requirement was not “a chatbot.” It was one reliable system to run the front and back office, that was:

Safe to put in front of vulnerable customers

Never invents a price, a policy, or availability. Escalates anything sensitive to a human.

Cheap to run, and easy for a non-technical owner to operate and trust

One deployment, one bill, and an interface an owner can actually read — not a developer tool.

Multi-channel, sharing one brain

Phone, email, calendar and outbound all grounded in the same knowledge base, so facts never diverge between channels.

The Agent Pipeline

Each stage does one job

Every inbound email flows through a multi-stage pipeline where each stage has a single responsibility — the pattern that makes the AI safe rather than a black box.

01

Triage fast, cheap model

Classifies route and sensitivity, with a deterministic high-risk keyword check that runs BEFORE the LLM, so safety never depends on the model getting it right.

02

Resolve strong model

Drafts a reply grounded only in the knowledge base, citing the source of every factual claim. Hedges instead of guessing.

03

Critic same tier as resolve

Verifies each claim against the knowledge base using weakest-claim scoring — the reply’s score is its lowest claim, not the average — and checks it speaks in the right voice. Nothing is auto-sent unverified.

04

Tiered decision

Routine and fully-grounded → auto-send. Pricing or sensitive → warm acknowledgement to the customer plus immediate escalation to the owner. Low-confidence → queued for one-click approval, batched into a periodic digest. Spam → ignored.

This is what makes it deployable in a careful, vulnerable-person context: it is grounded, verified, and human-in-the-loop by design — it knows what it does not know.

Engineering Highlights

The hard parts

Real systems are defined by the problems solved, not the happy path.

Grounded, anti-hallucination generation

A triage → draft → critic pipeline with cite-or-don’t-say rules and weakest-claim scoring, so the assistant never fabricates a price or policy — essential for a regulated, vulnerable-customer domain.

Resilient email transport across a hostile network

The host provider throttled the cloud’s IP on IMAP and the platform blocked all outbound SMTP ports. Diagnosed with a purpose-built connection-test endpoint, then re-architected: read via Gmail IMAP (forwarded), send via an HTTPS email API, with domain SPF/DKIM so mail still sends from the branded address — and forced IPv4 to dodge a missing-IPv6 route.

Self-healing background worker

The framework’s startup hook proved unreliable in production, so the worker self-heals from a health-check request, and the health endpoint surfaces poll count and last error — turning silent failures into a 30-second diagnosis. Instrument first, hypothesise second.

Voice with a real human handoff

A hybrid voice architecture — assistant in the voice platform, live facts served from the app via a tool — so business facts stay editable without redeploys. Warm transfer with a spoken summary, and a voicemail-detection fallback so a ring-out to voicemail is treated as a failed transfer and the AI takes a message instead of stranding the caller.

Booking that cannot double-book

Calendar tools re-check availability at write time and only ever touch events the agent created (tagged), never the owner’s own diary — and degrade gracefully to taking a message if the calendar is unreachable.

Cost-aware reporting for free

Daily briefings and call summaries reuse work already paid for — provider-generated summaries, database counts — so rich reporting adds effectively nothing to running cost. A deliberate architectural choice.

Design & Product

Transparency as a feature

The staff portal is intentionally premium and restrained — editorial serif headings, a mostly-neutral surface with a single muted-gold accent, hairline borders, generous whitespace, hierarchy from type not colour. It is a product a non-technical owner trusts, not a developer tool.

Every action carries a timestamp, the AI’s reasoning, and its cost. That transparency is also the answer to the owner’s core fear: “will AI embarrass me in front of my customers?”
