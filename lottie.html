Case Study · 01

CarePath AI

Voice and chat enquiry handling for a single-operator transport business

The same problem Eliza solves: the person who should answer the phone cannot, and every unanswered enquiry goes to whoever picks up first. CarePath handles the call, the email and the chat, quotes from a real coverage and pricing model, books the journey, and hands off to the owner only when a human genuinely needs to decide. Two decisions I would bring to this role: the safety gate is code, not a prompt — it runs before any model call and can only raise risk, never lower it — and the retrieval confidence floor sits at 0.59, measured in the gap between legitimate queries (0.61–0.86) and irrelevant ones (0.53–0.56). When a genuine question fell below it, I added the missing document rather than lowering the floor. 670 tests, all running offline. Read the full case study →

Case Study · 02

AI Operations System

UK healthcare & patient transport operator — live in production

A live production system answering real calls and real email for a business serving elderly, disabled and SEND passengers — callers for whom a wrong answer is not a bad user experience but a genuine harm. It answers, books, escalates, and reports to the owner daily. The parts relevant here: a warm voice handoff with a spoken summary, plus voicemail detection so a ring-out is treated as a failed transfer rather than stranding the caller; a triage → resolve → critic pipeline where nothing is auto-sent unverified; and tiered escalation, so pricing and safeguarding always reach a human while routine enquiries do not.
